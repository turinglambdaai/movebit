Features

Reminders you can't click away.

The usual tools pop a little bubble and you dismiss it without standing up. MoveBit takes the commit-out-of-it approach seriously.

Forced break, every monitor

A topmost, full-screen break lock with countdown appears on every connected display. Alt+F4-proof; the skip button only shows after a delay, so skipping is deliberate, not a reflex.

Measures active work time

Session-wide idle detection watches keyboard and mouse across all apps. Leave your desk and the clock stops.

Micro breaks, no lock

Every 30 minutes a gentle screen-center nudge — stand up, look far away for 20 seconds. No lock, no sound, Esc to dismiss.

History that survives restarts

Today's stats plus a last-7-days chart: active minutes per day, break counts, all persisted to disk.

Water reminders stay toasts

Hydration runs on its own cycle as a lightweight bottom-right toast — no need to lock the screen just to take a sip.

Pause for an hour

Meetings and screen sharing get a one-hour pause from the tray menu.

Light & dark, follow the system

Paper-warm for the workday, warm-dark for night use — the theme follows your system automatically.

Autostarts, quietly

A health tool only works if it's running. One toggle in settings registers MoveBit to start with your login.

Everything configurable

Intervals, break duration, away threshold, skip delay, sound — all editable in the settings window.

How it works

Three cycles, one honest clock.

Sit cycle 45 min

Accumulates only while you're active. At the interval it fires a full-screen break lock (default 5 min) when forced breaks are on, otherwise a toast.

Micro break cycle 30 min

A screen-center nudge to stand and look far away for 20 seconds — no lock, no sound.

Water cycle 30 min

Runs on its own interval as a lightweight bottom-right toast.

Stepped away? All cycles reset. Idle beyond the away threshold (default 5 min) means you already took the break — every cycle restarts from zero when you return.
